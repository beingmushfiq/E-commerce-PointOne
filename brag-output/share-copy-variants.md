Modern commerce platforms typically force a false choice: an inflexible multi-tenant SaaS walled-garden with vendor lock-in, or fragile open-source plugins that lack operational rigor and accounting balance.

CENTERPOINT eliminates that compromise.

• 3-Panel Visual Store Studio & sandboxed CMS
